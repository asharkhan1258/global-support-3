Why Monitor Your Trademark?

Online trademark and logo infringement is becoming increasingly common. Unauthorized use of your brand assets can affect your reputation, customer trust, and revenue. While registering your trademarks and service marks is an important step, ongoing monitoring is essential to protect your intellectual property.

Once your trademark is registered, it is important to monitor the marketplace and take action against unauthorized use. Staying aware of potential infringements across the regions where your products or services are offered helps safeguard your brand and maintain your legal rights.
